04 / INTERPRETATION

What this lab proves

Standalone

Fluid and Rapier retain their independent public demos. The Osmocean-hosted Ultra-Water build is generated from a pinned upstream revision with a Rapier 3D floater integration applied during the build. Its build and deployment are verified; automated browser inspection cannot access the live WebGL scene reliably.

Co-resident

Multiple independent renderers can be opened at once. This exposes browser compatibility, GPU memory pressure, frame pacing and mobile thermal limits.

Coupled

Partially implemented for Ultra-Water + Rapier inside one runtime. The other pairings remain independent. Full two-way fluid coupling still requires shared state, explicit units and coordinates, conservative force exchange, and validation.
